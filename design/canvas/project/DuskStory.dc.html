Houna dusk

Dusk’s counterpart to the Night starfield and the Houna sunrise: the evening between them. Tapping Home’s mark sets the day aside, the light turns to golden hour, and the mark glides down, as the starfield’s moon does, settling low as a setting sun and becoming an amber sun, as the sky deepens to violet dusk and the first stars come out. Tap the sun to come back. The prototype beside this board plays it.

4Breathe
The evening sun breathes as the starfield’s moon does: its soft glow swells on the in-breath and mostly fades on the out, the disc and mark still. No rays: an evening sun is a glow. The first faint stars come out high in the violet, with the starfield’s shooting stars now and then, a nod to Night next door. “Tanafas” shows for three seconds. Tap the sun to go back.
One scene, two skiesBuilt on the sunrise: app/sunrise.tsx becomes the sun scene for both themes, reading its skies and sun from the theme (sunriseScene / a new duskScene in theme.ts). Same handoff, glide, breath and return; nothing new to wire.
Dusk theme onlyNight: the starfield. Sunrise: the sunrise. Dusk: this. Every theme’s mark now opens a scene.
One change to SunDiscSunDisc takes the sun’s colours and whether it has rays: Dusk’s is an amber disc, a rose-amber halo and a warm sunglow, breathing on the shared 5s breath, with no rays.
Counts as breathingA visit counts like the others: a dusk session, titled Tanafas in Recap, into streaks and the leaderboard.
DecidedThe mark stays inside the sun, in deep teal. The first faint stars come out once it settles. The sun settles lower than the moon and the sunrise (55% down the screen, not 42%); if that doesn’t feel right, it’s one value to bring it back in line with them.
