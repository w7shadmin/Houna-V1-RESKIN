NOTES

Before shipping

Things to settle with each edition.

Saudi Arabia uses its coloursThe Saudi flag carries the Shahada, and Saudi rules keep it out of trademarks and commercial use and forbid altering it; a waving, cropped flag in an app icon would be both. Brands there mark National Day with the green and white, as this edition does, or with the official National Day identity.
Flags are simplifiedColours follow each flag’s official values. Oman’s emblem is left out because it’s too fine to read at icon size. For launch, each flag should be checked against the state’s own specification.
How it switchesiOS can change icons in-app (alternate icons), but asks the person to confirm each time. Android uses one launcher alias per icon. Either could be an opt-in “App icon” choice in Profile, rather than changing on its own.
