HOUNA · STARFIELD

Houna moon — concepts

New directions for the starfield’s moon, each breathing on the 5s rhythm as it would in the scene. The first two are for reference: the original glowing logo, and the pressed teal moon in the app today. The rest are new.

OriginalREFERENCE
The glowing teal logo, its halo breathing from the logo’s edge.
Pressed tealIN THE APP
A small solid teal moon, the logo pressed in: today’s moon.
Crescent cradleNEW
A thin lit crescent curving round the glowing logo, as if it rests in the moon.
PhasesNEW
The moon waxes to full on the in-breath and wanes to a crescent on the out-breath: the breath drawn in its light.
EclipseNEW
A dark moon with a teal corona breathing round it, the logo glowing on its face.
CrateredNEW
A soft moonlit disc with faint craters, the logo pressed in as the largest of them.
ConstellationNEW
The logo traced in stars joined by faint lines, twinkling: no disc, the moon made of the sky itself.
Line moonNEW
A fine luminous outline of the moon and the logo, drawn like the app’s icons; the glow breathes.
RipplesNEW
A small solid moon sending soft rings outward on each breath out, like a pebble in still water.
GibbousNEW
A side-lit sphere with a real day–night edge; the logo pressed into the lit side.
Moon haloNEW
The small pressed moon inside a wide, faint atmospheric ring, the kind that circles the moon on a clear cold night.
PearlNEW
An iridescent moon, its teal, lavender and rose sheen turning slowly; the logo pressed in.
