Houna · Sadu Sadu in Houna Al Sadu is the Bedouin weaving of Kuwait and the Gulf, on UNESCO’s list of intangible heritage: warp-faced bands of small stepped cells, border motifs round a central tree, in black, brown, beige and red brightened with orange. Here it’s woven into the places Houna already has: the sun’s rays, the breathing stage, the words round the sun and moon, the splash, the Tanafas scenes and the app’s small surfaces, with Kuwait Towers, Liberation Tower, the Grand Mosque and a boom only now and then. Every board moves where the idea is motion. Pick by selecting boards or commenting on one.
