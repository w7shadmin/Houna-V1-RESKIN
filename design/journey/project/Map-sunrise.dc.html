Houna · the journey map · Sunrise

Eight moments, one calm thread

Stage 1 Arrive To feel something before being asked for anything. One guided breath comes first: no sign-up, no questions. Then Home, where others are breathing too.
Curious, then settled.
Stage 2 Choose a sky To make it feel like theirs. Three skies, Sunrise, Dusk and Night; the wordmark is the switch. Each sky’s body opens a breathing scene of its own.
Personal, and a little magical.
Stage 3 Breathe To calm down, now. Five exercises in Tanafas, each with its own stage. A gentle countdown, then the screen goes quiet: the bars hide and the controls step aside.
Held, never rushed.
Stage 4 Meditate To take ten minutes somewhere else. Four scenes with sound and video, any length, chosen in place on glass sheets. The player is a dark, quiet focus mode.
Away, and safe to be away.
Stage 5 Reflect To understand how they feel. A mood check-in in a glass sheet, a private journal on the phone (exported as a PDF), and short self-reflection questionnaires, with “not a diagnosis” said first.
Honest, private, never judged.
Stage 6 Find support To reach a real person. A directory of therapists, organisations and centres, searched on the phone in either language, and events to join. Crisis help is one tap from Home.
Not alone, and not lost.
Stage 7 See the practice To see it adding up, without pressure. Your sky and your month in breath on Profile, the month in moons, a monthly Recap, badges, and for an Alias, weekly stats and a gentle leaderboard. Streaks count practice, never mood.
Proud, quietly.
Stage 8 In Arabic To feel it was made in their language. Everything in Arabic and English, laid out right to left, with Arabic type and Arabic-Indic numerals, and Arabic plural agreement.
At home.
How it should feel
