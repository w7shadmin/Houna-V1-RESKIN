I4An orrery
Each badge a small orrery round a sun: an orbit and a planet for 3 days, two for 7, three for 14; at 30 a ringed planet half in shadow, and at 100 the whole system with a comet crossing. Exploring: a comet for the first breath (something beginning); five planets in the five tones sharing one orbit; four on four orbits in the scenes’ colours; an eclipse for every sky, the moon across the sun with its corona. The planets turn slowly on their orbits (on the badges page and the unlock only; still under Reduce Motion). Not yet: dashed orbits and hollow planets, the system drawn but not lit. Profile: a planet on its orbit, a ringed planet, an eclipse.
