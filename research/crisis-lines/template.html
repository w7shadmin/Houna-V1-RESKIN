MENA Crisis Lines خطوط الأزمات

Every emergency number, crisis and suicide line, and mental-health support service we could find in 18 Arab countries, with the source each one came from. It is a draft for clinical and local review before any of it goes into the app: numbers marked Likely or Uncertain need a phone check first.

What stands out

  • Only a few places have a 24/7 line dedicated to mental-health crises: Lebanon (Embrace 1564), Abu Dhabi (800 725462, 800-SAKINA), Palestine (Sawa 164) and Egypt (16328, 24/7 per 2026 reports).
  • Daytime-only national lines: Saudi Arabia (920 033 360), Qatar (16000, option 4, closed Fridays), Tunisia (80 105 050, until 14:30), Dubai (800 506, to midnight). The app should always show the emergency number beside them.
  • No national mental-health or suicide line was confirmed in Morocco, Algeria, Bahrain, Oman, Iraq, Syria, Sudan or Libya. There, the emergency, child and family lines are the routes; in Oman and Kuwait, the psychiatric hospital's 24/7 emergency line (24873268, 24621000).
  • Several widely copied numbers are wrong or dead: Sawa's old 121, Morocco's Stop Silence (closed 2025), COVID-era lines across the Gulf, and made-up Syria and Sudan numbers on aggregator sites. Each country lists them under "Don't use".
  • In Gaza, Sudan, Yemen, Syria and Libya every line depends on networks and funding. Recheck these every few months.

How this was checked

Six researchers each covered a group of countries, opening every source listed here. Official pages came first: ministries, government portals, the services' own sites, and UN agency help pages. Where a government site refused the connection, dated national press reporting the ministry was used instead.

  • Verified On an official or operator page that is current.
  • Likely On a reputable recent source that isn't the operator's own page, or official but with details that are older or disputed.
  • Uncertain Old, conflicting, or found only on aggregator lists. Don't publish until someone has called it.

Before any of this reaches the app: a clinician should review the crisis and support lines, and someone in each country should call every Likely and Uncertain line during its stated hours. Short codes (three to five digits) work only when dialled inside that country. Store a "last checked" date with each line and recheck every six months, sooner for conflict areas.
